About

Glazer Construction was built from a lifelong passion for construction.

Menachem grew up working in his father's construction company from a young age before establishing Glazer Construction in 2023. He is passionate about detailed, quality work and specializes in residential construction for homeowners.

Glazer Construction also takes on commercial fit-outs and multi-family construction, serving homeowners, investors, and landlords alike, with quality construction and your budget in mind.
